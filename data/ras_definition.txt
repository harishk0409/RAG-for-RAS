What is IEEE RAS?

IEEE RAS stands for the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society. It is a professional society within IEEE focused on the theory, practice, and advancement of robotics and automation engineering and science.

The IEEE RAS VIT Chennai Student Chapter is the local chapter of this society at VIT Chennai, providing students an environment to grow their skills in robotics and automation under the guidance of IEEE professionals and industry experts, and to stay updated on the latest trends and research in the field.
