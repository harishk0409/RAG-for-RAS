Event: RoverX — The Hardware Workshop 2025
Date: 31 October – 2 November 2025
Context: Part of TechnoVIT, VIT Chennai's national-level technology festival

RoverX was a hands-on hardware workshop organised by the IEEE RAS VIT Chennai
Student Chapter, teaching participants to build DIY rovers and robotic systems
using the ESP32 microcontroller platform. It was framed as an opportunity for
students to engage in creative, real-world problem-solving in robotics and
automation. The chapter invited sponsorship from companies working in robotics,
automation, AI, AGVs, AMRs, and humanoid systems, including outreach to partners
such as Zerlon Semi Pvt. Ltd., a Coimbatore-based semiconductor training institute.

(Source: IEEE RAS Student Chapter VIT Chennai, LinkedIn page)
