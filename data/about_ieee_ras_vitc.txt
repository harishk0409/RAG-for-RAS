IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) — VIT Chennai Student Chapter

The IEEE RAS VIT Chennai Student Chapter operates under the School of Electronics
Engineering at VIT Chennai (Vandalur–Kelambakkam Road, Chennai 600127), alongside
VIT Chennai's wider IEEE Student Branch, which also hosts the IEEE Computer Society,
IEEE Signal Processing Society, and IEEE Women in Engineering affinity group.

IEEE RAS globally is a branch of IEEE with roughly 140,000 members across 160
countries, operating more than 80 local networks. More than 220 local and regional
RAS chapters are active worldwide, sponsoring mini-symposia, student competitions,
and continuing-education workshops. The Society's objectives are scientific,
literary, and educational: advancing the theory and practice of robotics and
automation engineering and science, and maintaining high professional standards
among its members.

The VIT Chennai chapter was inaugurated at the VIT Chennai campus with a welcome
address from faculty coordinator Dr. Suchetha M, Associate Professor, School of
Electronics Engineering. Dr. Rajasekaran, Assistant Director of Student Welfare at
VIT Chennai, attended as guest of honour, and the Society's then Vice President,
Mohammed Abdullah, spoke about IEEE's mission of advancing technology for humanity.
