Event: IoT in Robotics — Interactive Demo Session
Date: 19 December 2018
Venue: VIT Chennai campus

An interactive demo session on "IoT in Robotics" was conducted as part of the
chapter's regular activities. The session was led by industry expert Ms. Vasanthi
of SANDS India Private Ltd, and opened with an introduction to IEEE and IEEE RAS
from faculty coordinator Dr. Suchetha M. It covered the intersection of two major
technology trends — the Internet of Things and robotics — an area of active
interest across the VIT Chennai student body considering robotics and automation
as a future field of study.

(Source: VIT Chennai, "IEEE RAS VITCC — Chapter Initiative Grant" attachment,
chennai.vit.ac.in)
